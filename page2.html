Data & Assumptions

  • Data source: data/traffic_incidents.json (30 incidents, 12 roads)
  • Time horizon: snapshot of all incidents present in the dataset; "Active" incidents drive all KPIs and delay totals.
  • Hotspot definition: road with the highest total estimated_delay_min across its Active incidents.
  • Lanes affected: sum of lanes_affected for Active incidents on that road only.
  • Demo notice: this is synthetic demonstration data — not real LTA or live traffic data.
